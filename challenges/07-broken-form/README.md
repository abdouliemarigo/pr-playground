# Challenge 07: Broken Form

**Difficulty:** Beginner+  
**Skill focus:** HTML forms, careful reading, verifying in the browser

## The problem

Open `index.html`. Fill in the contact form and click **Send**. Nothing useful happens — the form should navigate to `thanks.html` and show a success message.

## Your task

1. Find the HTML issue(s) preventing the form from submitting correctly.
2. Fix `index.html` so submitting the form takes you to `thanks.html`.
3. Confirm the flow works in the browser.
4. Commit and open a PR explaining what was wrong and how you tested it.

**Hint:** Look at the form tag and the submit control carefully. Do not change `thanks.html`.
